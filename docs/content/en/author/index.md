This guide is for the people who work with content: authors, editors, translators, the people who look after media, and the administrators who set things up for them. It needs no technical knowledge.

If you have not used Sensible Studio before, take the [tour of the interface](../getting-started/tour.md) first. It takes five minutes and the rest of this guide assumes it.

## Pages

:::cards
- [Pages](pages.md): Browse, create, move, copy and delete pages. Versions, the timeline, and bringing deleted pages back.
- [Page properties](page-properties.md): Titles, tags, when a page is visible, short addresses, search engine settings, permissions.
- [The page editor](page-editor.md): Edit in place, add and move components, lay out for each screen size, annotate, preview.
- [Templates](templates.md): What new pages start with, which parts are fixed, and which components authors may add.
:::

- [Experience Fragments](experience-fragments.md): Parts of pages written once and shown in many places.
